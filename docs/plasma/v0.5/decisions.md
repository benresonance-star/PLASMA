## Precedence and lifecycle
A new thread proposes a change against the exact baseline commit. Record source, rationale, affected contracts, backward compatibility, migration and acceptance gate.
Statuses: proposed → incorporated-in-candidate → accepted-baseline → implemented → supported-by-evidence → qualified-for-stated-scope. These are separate dimensions in implementation.md; progression in one dimension does not imply progression in another.
Superseding a decision does not rewrite evidence or migrate project baselines automatically.
Unresolved contradictions remain in this register; do not choose a newest filename as implicit authority.
