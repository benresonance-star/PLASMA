Constitution

  1. CONST-001 — Canonical world state has one governed authority path.
  2. CONST-002 — A proposal is never authoritative state.
  3. CONST-003 — Derived representations cannot become a second source of truth.
  4. CONST-004 — Stale computation cannot overwrite a newer authoritative revision.
  5. CONST-005 — Failure of a candidate operation cannot corrupt accepted state.
  6. CONST-006 — Evidence retains provenance, applicability and freshness.
  7. CONST-007 — History is preserved through revision-producing events.
  8. CONST-008 — Non-authoritative computation may guide authority but cannot silently become authority.
